ApexMart Site Plan

Site Purpose

The site provides a modern e-commerce platform where customers can browse featured products, filter items across categories, view detailed specifications, and seamlessly manage a persistent shopping cart for a smooth mock checkout experience.

Target Audience Scenarios

Visitors can click directly on any product card or navigate to the catalog page to view detailed specifications, prices, and stock status.

Users can utilize category filters on the shop page and access the cart page/drawer anytime via the top navigation bar.

Color Scheme

The site utilizes a clean, modern palette suitable for tech and retail branding:

  • Primary Navy (#0f172a): Header navigation background, main titles, primary buttons, and footer.
  • Accent Royal Blue (#2563eb): Interactive hover effects, price tags, badge highlights, and active links.
  • Neutral Background (#f8fafc): Global body background color and subtle borders.
  • Card Surface (#ffffff): Product cards, checkout form boxes, and modal backgrounds.

Typography

  • Heading Font: Montserrat, sans-serif — Used for all page headings (H1, H2, H3), logo text, and primary call-to-action buttons.
  • Body Font: Open Sans or Roboto, sans-serif — Used for main body text, product details, paragraph descriptions, and form inputs.
